Welcome to Karnataka

Karnataka is a beautiful state in South India. It is famous for its historical monuments, beautiful waterfalls, green hill stations, beaches and delicious food. It is a perfect destination for travellers and nature lovers.

Famous Tourist Places

Mysore Palace

A beautiful royal palace known for its architecture and history.

Hampi

Explore ancient temples and the historical ruins of Hampi.

Coorg

Enjoy coffee plantations, greenery and peaceful landscapes.

Historical Places

Badami Caves

Famous for ancient rock-cut caves and beautiful architecture.

Belur Temple

Known for its detailed stone carvings and temple architecture.

Gol Gumbaz

A famous historical monument located in Vijayapura.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

Jog Falls

One of India's famous waterfalls surrounded by nature.

Chikmagalur

A beautiful hill station famous for coffee estates and hills.

Agumbe

Known for green forests, beautiful views and heavy rainfall.

Famous Karnataka Foods

Mysore Masala Dosa

A popular crispy dosa with spicy chutney and potato filling.

Idli Vada

A traditional breakfast served with chutney and sambar.

Bisi Bele Bath

A delicious traditional rice dish with lentils and spices.
